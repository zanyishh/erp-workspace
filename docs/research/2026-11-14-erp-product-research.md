# Where ERP Workspace can earn a daily user's preference

14 November 2026. These recommendations come from public product documentation, the pinned ERPNext implementation and this project's isolated rehearsals. Competitor documentation describes capabilities. It does not establish which interface is faster or easier for this project's customers.

| Work to make easier | What the sources show | Product decision and how to test it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finding the right screen | Business Central lets people search for pages and tasks in context and bookmark frequent destinations. [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search) | Make the catalogue understand phrases a clerk actually uses, such as “receive goods” and “bill customer”. On a first-use trial, give staff a business task without naming the screen. Record whether they find it unassisted, how long it takes and their first wrong turn. Compare results against exact record-name search. |
| Knowing what needs attention | Business Central's role centres can show counts linked to actions, such as open invoices. [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-cues-action-tiles) | Prioritise an actionable queue for each role. Test a clerk opening a late receipt or unpaid invoice from the count. Confirm that the list and count agree for the same company, date and permissions. |
| Moving material through a workshop | ERPNext tracks material transfer, work in progress, production and finished stock against Work Orders. Its operation and job-card paths have additional rules. [ERPNext Work Order](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/work-order) | Keep the order attached to each stock movement. Test a partial transfer, partial completion and reopening the order. Confirm units and warehouses in ERPNext and check what the next shift sees. |
| Receiving stock with a scanner | Odoo supports opening a receipt, scanning items and reviewing quantities before validation. [Odoo 19 receipts and deliveries](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/operations/receipts_deliveries.html) | Test one real scanner and one phone against an expected receipt, with duplicate scans, an unknown item and a wrong destination. Measure time per line and the number of corrections. Typing or rendering a barcode alone cannot validate this workflow. |
| Settling open invoices | ERPNext payment reconciliation links an existing payment or credit to an invoice. Bank reconciliation separately matches a bank transaction with posted vouchers. [ERPNext payment reconciliation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-reconciliation) · [ERPNext bank reconciliation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/bank-reconciliation) | Label these as separate tasks. Show the party, account, currency, remaining amount and proposed allocation together. A successful rehearsal includes a partial payment, a stale second attempt and an independent read of both resulting balances. |
| Recovering from mistakes | W3C's guidance calls for understandable, actionable error messages and clear feedback after success. [W3C form notifications](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/) | Test recovering from a rejected quantity without retyping the other fields. With a screen reader and a hardware keyboard, check that the user can find the affected row, hear the correction and confirm the outcome. |

## First comparative trial

Recruit a business owner, accounts clerk, storekeeper and mill operator who have not used this app. Give each person the same starting records and intended outcome in the current app and a reference workflow they are authorised to use. Alternate the order to reduce learning bias. Use synthetic records. Record completion without coaching, time to first correct action, time to finish, errors, corrections and the final ledger or custody result. Repeat at desktop and phone sizes. Ask what the participant expected at their first wrong turn.

The first three tasks should be receiving a partial supplier order, applying part of an incoming payment and handing a knitted roll from inspection to dispatch. They exercise stock, money and custody, and each has a result that can be independently checked. Treat wrong-company records, duplicate postings and incorrect balances as release blockers. Set speed targets after collecting a real baseline; a local render benchmark measures a different outcome.
