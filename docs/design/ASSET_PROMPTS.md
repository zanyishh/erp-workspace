Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the approved general ERP reference-led UI. Original generated PNGs are saved in apps/erp_client/assets/images/. No reference branding or photographic screenshot was copied into the product.

## warehouse

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a premium ERP dashboard's large hero object, a semi-realistic white 3D product render. A single elegant contemporary warehouse operations diorama: a low wide white warehouse with ribbed white cladding, charcoal loading-bay recesses, a partially open roller door revealing neatly stacked white cartons, and a few white cartons on a very pale grey pallet just outside. One small pale warm-peach architectural accent only. Entire structure visible, isolated, very wide horizontal composition viewed from a shallow front three-quarter angle, close to eye-level, NOT a steep isometric game view. White-on-white architectural product photography with fine realistic edges, subtle ambient occlusion, soft front/top studio lighting, broad very soft grey contact shadow preserved beneath the object. Restrained monochrome palette: chalk white, very light neutral grey, charcoal details, minuscule muted peach. High-end industrial catalogue/Cinema4D render, quiet precise materials. Plenty of transparent padding, object occupies middle 80 percent of a wide landscape canvas. Actual transparent background including feathered transparent shadow. No ground slab, no huge base platform, no truck, no people, no plants, no text, no logos, no watermark. This must look like a real softly lit white industrial model rather than an emoji, toy or colourful illustration.

## Studio library

The purchasing render above was the visual reference for six original transparent objects. Each uses the same near-eye-level catalogue camera, chalk-white surfaces, graphite details, muted peach trim and soft contact shadow. The subjects are yarn cones, a circular knitting machine, rolled jersey fabric, a fabric inspection station, a dispatch truck and a calculator with invoice forms. Their source and output checksums are in `asset-provenance.json`.

The client bundles the six renders alongside optimized copies of the original warehouse, point-of-sale terminal and purchasing trolley. Each image has 144, 448 and 960 pixel WebP versions under `assets/studio/`. Table headers use compact symbols and screen-reader labels, while quantities, dates and customer names stay in text.

The general manufacturing illustration is a chalk-white assembly robot holding a small metal component over a fixture. The original purchasing trolley supplied the lighting and camera reference. A muted peach band and graphite joint rings distinguish the tool at small sizes. Work Orders use this object. The circular knitting machine belongs to the mill. The same three WebP sizes are bundled for both.

## Invoice stationery

The November 2026 invoice redesign uses original studio imagery. The generative briefs specified a cobalt and copper folded ribbon on black, violet silk, terracotta architectural stairs, sage leaf folds, a burgundy selvedge, cyanotype ocean water and pearlescent shell. The bundled crops are in `apps/erp_client/assets/invoice_art/`. All text, amounts, brand marks uploaded by users, grids and payment codes are placed separately by the PDF renderer. The printed display face is an 800-weight instance of the bundled OFL Manrope font. Reference images were used for composition and art direction.
