## Test history and limits

Retained logs show failing-before-fix cases for staff writes, split/QC cancellation, Desk privacy, submitted-source guarding, lifecycle transitions, subcontract commitment, physical counts, shifts, challan/QR output, breakage attribution, partial auxiliary invoice precision and the final native confirmation. Other cases were added as regression checks during the fix pass and first passed; a failing-before-fix run is not evidenced for every review item. In particular `review-access-red.log` is a historical filename whose two tests actually passed. It must not be cited as a failed test run. The 18 dispositions therefore do not imply 18 independently observed RED→GREEN histories.

Native verification covers the recorded synthetic cycle and document links only. Windows, physical devices/signing, customer migration, vendor-specific formulas, provider submissions, hardware, full-site recovery, large-factory throughput and motion frame profiling remain explicit gates.
