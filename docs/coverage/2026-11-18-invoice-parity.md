# Invoice workflow comparison

**The Flutter app can create a server-reviewed draft, submit it through the full editor and print A4, A5, 58 mm or 80 mm output. It does not yet reproduce the Swift app’s design editor or its issued-PDF archive.** In the Flutter implementation reviewed, changing the company artwork or payment settings can change a later reprint of an already submitted invoice. The first acceptance case should issue a synthetic invoice, change company settings and record a payment, then confirm the original issued PDF is still byte-for-byte retrievable.

Reviewed the current Swift sources in the sibling `INVOICE/Invoices` checkout and the Flutter feature, native document editor and Frappe endpoints in this working tree. This is a source comparison of ordinary sales invoicing. Imported Knitpack original prints use a separate read-only projection. No customer instance, source application account or customer document was opened.

## Operator verification on 18 November

Typing an item identifier one character at a time reproduced a focus jump after the first character. The composer now advances after an explicit field submission or lookup selection. The keyboard lookup accepts a unique match after the current search resolves. An account-access refresh between a save receipt and its confirmation frame also exposed a stale snackbar over the recovery button. The invalidated confirmation now closes, keeping the operator's status check available.

The compact invoice register keeps Invoices in the toolbar. The company name, register actions, tabs and search scroll away together as the operator browses records. On phones, amounts and status use the same right edge on every row, including when that row has no print permission. The desktop register retains aligned fixed columns. Both use the payable amount after applicable rounding and preserve the server's decimal digits.

The pinned Flutter engine passed five composer entry cases, ten register cases, ten invoice UX cases, four register scope cases, twenty-four composer model cases and eight composer scope/recovery cases. Eight current renders covered the register and populated composer on iPhone, Android and desktop viewports, plus both phones with a simulated open keyboard. Visual inspection identified and corrected the mobile amount-column drift and the desktop due-date control width. Synthetic backend checks covered recent-customer order, duplicate suppression, eligibility, masked fields and company scope. Real database tests for recent customers and rounded register totals are checked in. Draft text before the first save has no encrypted local recovery store. Server-side recovery of an uncertain write is a separate, exercised path.

Three Swift synthetic executables passed in this review. `TemplateCatalogChecks` confirmed 15 current designs and historical decoding. `QuickBillingChecks` confirmed item identity, customer pricing, atomic template application and guided entry. `PrintOutputChecks` generated seven PDFs and verified all four paper sizes, the immutable issued A4 bytes, cancellation marking, mixed units, long text and fractional rates. Compilation, module caches and generated PDFs were written to a temporary directory outside the Swift checkout.

The initial comparison was read-only. A subsequent Flutter source pass restored line ordering and keyboard progression, aligned quantity and rate validation with the server’s decimal limits, and applied the PDF decimal formatter to review and confirmation totals. Real-device interaction, printer output and an end-to-end ERP journey through actual posting, payment and reprint were outside this comparison.

The phone company-settings screen keeps Save settings above the keyboard. Seven pinned settings cases passed, including saving a signatory and clearing the footer. A synthetic schema-upgrade and legacy-client exercise preserved these values across older clients. The connected schema upgrade remains unverified.

## Knitting billing integration

The preserved KnitPack catalogue lists separate top-level Masters and Accounts menus. It lists Invoice under Knitpack Order, General Invoice under Knitpack Accounts, and date, party and job variants of the historical invoice register. The ERP sidebar shortcut previously labelled Masters & accounts opens the permission-scoped company catalogue and now appears as Company records.

In the mill workspace, Billing contains the shared ERP sales register, delivery-linked job billing and invoice templates. Original KnitPack invoices open in the read-only history filtered to invoices. A normal bill uses the shared composer and opens the saved draft in the full ERP editor. A delivered-fabric bill uses the job's confirmed deliveries, requires an explicit tax decision and shows the server-calculated payable amount, including any rounding, before posting. The confirmed bill can open the shared PDF preview if the account can read and print it. The sales register can show the original job reference where the account has access to that job and field. Original delivery references remain visible in job billing with their source dates.

The original date-, party- and job-filtered reports and the Swift issued-PDF archive should be assessed independently of this navigation and totals change. This pass verified synthetic desktop and phone interactions. Database-backed posting against the isolated site still requires a running local ERPNext service.
